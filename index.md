# Privacy Policy for Kanji Lock

**Last updated: September 20, 2026**

Kanji Lock (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Kanji Lock.

## 1. Information We Collect

Kanji Lock does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The App stores certain information necessary to provide its features, including:

* Your selected JLPT levels and quiz settings
* Learning progress, answer history, and learning statistics
* Custom kanji cards you create or import
* App settings and onboarding status
* Information necessary to enable the app-blocking feature

This information is stored locally on your device or within the App Group storage used by Kanji Lock and its iOS extensions.

We do not operate a server that collects or stores this information.

## 2. Screen Time and App Blocking

Kanji Lock uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks, including Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity, to provide its app-blocking functionality.

When you select apps or app categories to block, the selection is handled using Apple's Family Controls framework.

Kanji Lock does not receive or store the names of the specific apps you select. The App receives an opaque representation provided by Apple that allows it to apply the requested restrictions without identifying the selected apps.

Information accessed through Apple's Screen Time APIs is used solely to provide Kanji Lock's app-blocking functionality.

## 3. Learning Data

Kanji Lock records information such as:

* Questions you have answered
* Whether your answers were correct or incorrect
* Learning counts and statistics
* Learning progress for individual kanji
* Recent learning history
* Custom kanji cards you create or import

This information is used to provide learning progress, statistics, quiz functionality, and other features within the App.

This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

## 4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Kanji Lock may offer paid subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Purchases and subscription management are processed by Apple using StoreKit. Kanji Lock may receive information from StoreKit about whether you have an active subscription and information necessary to display and provide subscription features.

We do not receive or store your payment card details or other payment information.

Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

## 5. Analytics and Advertising

Kanji Lock does not currently use third-party analytics services or advertising SDKs.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, cross-app tracking, or profiling.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Because Kanji Lock does not operate a backend service for storing your learning data, your learning history, custom cards, and app-blocking selections are not uploaded to our servers.

Information may be processed by Apple where necessary to provide operating system services, App Store purchases, subscriptions, and Screen Time functionality, subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Learning data and settings stored by Kanji Lock generally remain on your device until they are deleted through the App, the App's data is reset, or the App is removed from your device.

Because this information is stored locally and is not associated with a Kanji Lock account on our servers, we generally do not possess a server-side copy of your learning data to delete.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Kanji Lock is an educational application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to provide their name, email address, or other identifying information to use its core functionality.

## 9. Security

Kanji Lock is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of user information. Learning data and settings are primarily kept locally on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Kanji Lock's features or data-handling practices change.

If we make material changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.

## 11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Kanji Lock's handling of information, please contact us through the contact form provided within the Kanji Lock app.
